About Sunny Search

Sunny Search is built on SearXNG, an open-source metasearch engine that combines results from multiple providers while minimizing tracking. We do not build personal ad profiles, and we keep the interface clean, fast, and focused.

What makes it different?

  • Privacy-first search experience with fewer trackers and less clutter.
  • Multiple categories (General, Images, News, Videos, Map, Music, IT, Science, Files, Social Media) in one place.
  • Modern light/dark interface, fast keyboard-friendly navigation, and customizable preferences.

Our design goals

  • Readable, compact, and information-dense results.
  • Consistent behavior across desktop and mobile.
  • Transparent controls so you can adjust filters quickly.

Open source

Sunny Search is powered by SearXNG. If you want to learn more, contribute, or self-host, visit the SearXNG documentation.
